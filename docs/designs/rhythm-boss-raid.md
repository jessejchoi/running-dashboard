# Design: Rhythm + Boss Raid

Mode: Builder

## Problem Statement

RunDash needs one individual and one group gamification feature. The goal is to (a) keep people showing up consistently and (b) keep the running group alive.

The way consistency actually breaks is a **slow fade**: 4 runs a week drifts to 3, then 2, and nobody notices until a month has gone by. The only consistency metric the app has today, `computeStreak` (`lib/utils.ts:98`), counts consecutive weeks with **at least one run**. It can't see the fade: someone going from 4 runs a week to 1 keeps their streak.

The group's banter lives in a **WhatsApp group**, not in RunDash. RunDash is where people check stats.

## What Makes This Cool

- **Your consistency becomes a weapon.** Hit your own weekly Rhythm and your weapon upgrades, from Stick to Dagger, Sword, Axe and Legendary. Individual progress is something you can see and collect, not just a number.
- **The group fights a boss together.** Each week a majority must hit their Rhythm for the group's attack to land. Everyone who showed up deals damage with their weapon. A 4-week raid gives every week a stake and the month a storyline: slay the Humidity Golem, or watch it escape.
- **A fade gets noticed by your friends, not by an algorithm.** The Monday battle report names the MVP and the people who let the boss live, and gives them a way to redeem themselves. It's built to be posted in WhatsApp.

## Constraints

- Side project for Jesse and a small friend group. Keep it cheap to build and maintain.
- Stack: Next.js 14.2 App Router, Supabase (migrations pasted manually into the SQL editor), Strava data, Vercel.
- **Time zones are per runner.** In-progress work (`lib/timezone.ts`, `supabase/migration_023.sql`) buckets every run by its own local date through `runDay(run)` in `lib/calendar.ts`.
  - Each runner's Rhythm week is Mon–Sun in their own zone.
  - A group's raid week is Mon–Sun in the group's zone, which is `commonTimezone(member zones)`.
  - Never use date-fns ISO weeks.
- `groups.id` is `uuid`, so every foreign key to it must be `uuid`.
- Visuals follow DESIGN.md. **Pixel-art sprites** (weapons, bosses) are a new visual element. They're proposed as orange-on-black and need explicit sign-off before they're added to DESIGN.md (see Open Questions).
- WhatsApp has no easy bot API. Sharing happens by a person tapping share.

## Premises (agreed)

1. The individual feature catches a slow fade using the runner's own normal rhythm, not "at least one run".
2. The group feature must produce something worth posting in WhatsApp. A mechanic that only lives inside the app will be ignored.
3. Individual consistency feeds the group mechanic, so fading lets friends down and doesn't just cost you personal points.
4. Everything is measured in weeks. Rest days never break anything, and nothing rewards racing easy runs or padding junk miles.

## Cross-Model Perspective

Codex was unavailable: its configured model `gpt-6-sol` isn't supported on a ChatGPT account. A cold-read Claude subagent was used instead.

- **Coolest version:** a co-op "boss fight" instead of a leaderboard. There's a per-runner Rhythm baseline, and the group shares a Campfire sized by the share of members hitting their own Rhythm. The Sunday card shows faders as ghost avatars.
- **Key insight:** "nobody notices until it's a month." The pain is that nobody sees the drift, not the missed runs themselves. Build for visibility, not rewards.
- **Existing tools that get halfway:** Habitica party quests (a missed daily hurts the whole party) for the loop, and `next/og` (Satori) for rendering the card.
- **Weekend build:** `lib/rhythm.ts` backtested against a real fade, then an OG card route, a share button, and Rhythm status passed into the Coach edition.

Jesse took the "boss fight" framing literally and pushed it further than the subagent did. Streak length becomes attack power, and a majority must show up for the attack to land. That's the design below.

Where we diverged from the subagent:

- **The baseline has to be sticky.** An automatic 8-week median follows a slow fade down and goes quiet.
- **Shaming always comes with a way back.** No ghost-shaming without a redemption path.
- **Telegram bot:** skipped for now. A share button covers both apps.

## Approaches Considered

- **A: Rhythm target + weekly share card.** A manual target and a Monday recap image. Ruled out: the recap has no shared stakes, so premise 3 is barely met.
- **B: Rhythm + Campfire.** A shared fire, lit when 2/3 of members hit their Rhythm, plus a streak and a share card. This was chosen first, then **evolved into Boss Raid** at Jesse's suggestion. Campfire's "lit" became "the attack lands", and a boss's HP gives the weeks a place to add up.
- **C: Rhythm + Buddy Pact.** Rotating weekly pairs. Ruled out: odd group sizes, and one inactive member drags down a single buddy.
- **Raid rules: Jesse's original (linear power, full reset on a miss, no redemption).** Softened because long streaks snowball (veterans deal 25 damage while newcomers deal 1), and a full reset plus pure shaming tends to push out the person who is already fading. The chosen rules keep the stakes but add weapon tiers, a one-tier drop on a miss, and redemption.

Also dropped during brainstorming: XP and levels, badge collections and run bingo (they reward generic activity and don't catch a fade); squad drafts and bounties (heavier, and more competitive than cooperative).

## Recommended Approach: Rhythm + Boss Raid

### Key design choice: a weekly ledger, frozen after a grace period (decided in eng review)

Each runner gets one row per week in `rhythm_weeks`. Once a row is **frozen**, it's never rewritten. Only the weeks that are still open get computed live from runs.

```
            frozen ledger rows (never rewritten)             open weeks (computed live)
 ┌──────────┬──────────┬─────┬──────────┬──────────┐ ┌──────────────┬──────────────┐
 │ launch wk│  wk +1   │ ... │ wk N-2   │ wk N-1 ◄─┼─┤ last week    │ this week    │
 │ level 0  │ level 1  │     │ level 9  │ level 4  │ │ (in 2-day    │ (in progress)│
 └──────────┴──────────┴─────┴──────────┴──────────┘ │  grace)      │              │
      ▲ fold starts here, everyone Unarmed            └──────────────┴──────────────┘
                                                        ▲ start from latest frozen row,
                                                          fold forward using the last ~14
                                                          weeks of runs
```

- **Fold start:** `RHYTHM_LAUNCH_WEEK`, the Monday of the week the feature ships. Everyone starts **Unarmed** (`level = 0`), and nothing before launch counts toward weapons.
  - It comes from the **`RHYTHM_LAUNCH_WEEK` environment variable** (for example `2026-10-19`), not a hardcoded constant. That way a slipped deploy can't leave the date wrong.
  - **It's also the feature flag.** When it's unset, the Rhythm cell, the freeze job and the Raid panel all stay off. When it's set to a date that isn't a Monday, it's treated as unset and an error is logged.
  - **Deploy order:**
    1. Paste migration 024.
    2. Deploy.
    3. Set the variable in Vercel.
    4. Redeploy.
  - **Missing tables don't crash anything.** If the migration hasn't been pasted, Supabase returns `42P01` (table doesn't exist). The loader catches that, treats the feature as off, and logs an error, so neither the dashboard nor the cron crashes.
- **Rebuild path** (decided in eng review, answering the outside-voice concern that frozen rows also freeze bugs):
  - Every ledger row stores `rules_version`, matching `RHYTHM_RULES_VERSION` in `lib/rhythm.ts`.
  - `scripts/rebuild-rhythm.ts [athleteId|--all]` deletes the athlete's rows and refolds them from launch week, using their full run history through `fetchAllPages`.
  - For a rule fix or retune: bump the version, deploy, then run the rebuild. The loader logs a warning when it reads rows with an older `rules_version`.
- **Freezing:** a week freezes once 2 days have passed since it ended, in the runner's own zone. For a Jakarta runner, that's Wednesday 00:00. The grace period absorbs late watch and Strava uploads. Once frozen, a row is final.
- **Who freezes weeks:** `freezeRhythmWeeks(athleteId)` in `lib/rhythmLedger.ts`.
  - It's idempotent: it folds forward from the latest frozen row and upserts every week that's ready to freeze.
  - **It runs in its own daily cron, `/api/cron/rhythm-freeze`, over ALL athletes** that have a Strava token (outside-voice finding #3). It doesn't live inside `/api/cron/sync`, for two reasons:
    - `next_sync_athletes()` returns only the 20 least-recently-synced athletes (`supabase/migration_021.sql:284`).
    - The sync loop shares a 25-second deadline with the Strava calls.
  - The route uses the same `CRON_SECRET` check as `app/api/cron/sync/route.ts`. It's scheduled in `vercel.json` at `30 17 * * *` (00:30 Jakarta). Before adding it, check that the Vercel plan allows a third cron.
  - `loadRaidState(groupId)` also calls `freezeRhythmWeeks` for **every member** who is behind, before it computes anything. Members who have stopped opening the app, the ones who are fading, are frozen whenever anyone views the group.
  - It's also called on a runner's own dashboard load when their rows are behind. All three callers are safe because the freeze is idempotent.
- **Reads are bounded while the freezer keeps up:** a runner's last 26 frozen rows, plus their runs from the last ~14 weeks. The 14 weeks cover the open weeks plus the 12-week duration floor.
  - To be precise, the run window starts **12 weeks before the first unfrozen week**. If freezing falls behind for a runner (the cron skipped them and they haven't opened their dashboard), the live fold still comes out correct. It just reads more runs until the next freeze catches up.
- **Before launch:** the first freeze reads about 20 weeks of runs from before launch, only to compute launch week's suggested target (8 weeks of counts, each with its own 12-week floor).
- **What the ledger provides:** the Monday battle report and boss HP read frozen rows directly. That covers last week's attackers and power, and everyone's weapon at the start of the raid. Until last week freezes on Wednesday, the report and HP are marked **provisional** on screen.
- **Rows are inspectable:** you can open `rhythm_weeks` in Supabase and see exactly why someone is on a Dagger.

**Ledger table.** `rhythm_weeks(athlete_id bigint, week_start date, qualifying_runs int, target int, target_source text check (target_source in ('stored','provisional')), suggested int, state text check (state in ('on','short','zero','rest')), level int check (level between 0 and 12), level_before_miss int, redeemed boolean not null default false, rules_version int not null, frozen_at timestamptz not null default now(), primary key (athlete_id, week_start))`.

- `level` is capped at 12. Anything above that is Legendary either way.
- `suggested` is stored per week, so the provisional target (the highest suggestion over the last 26 weeks) comes straight from ledger rows.

Two things are accepted and never retroactively applied to frozen weeks:

- Excluding an old activity from analysis.
- A member leaving the group. They drop out of future raid weeks only.

### Individual: Rhythm + Weapon

**Qualifying run.** A run counts toward Rhythm for week W when all of these hold:

- It's a run activity. The activities query filters `.eq('type', 'Run')`, the same as every existing run query.
- It isn't `excluded_from_analysis`.
- Its `moving_time` is at least `max(15 min, 50% × median run moving_time over the 12 weeks ending the day before W starts)`.

Because the floor for week W only looks at data from before W, it stays fixed for that week. The floor blocks junk miles, and treadmill runs count.

**Target history.** A new table, `rhythm_targets(athlete_id bigint, effective_week date, target int check (target between 1 and 14), created_at timestamptz default now(), primary key (athlete_id, effective_week))`.

- The target for week W is the row with the latest `effective_week` that is on or before W.
- **Setting or raising** the target takes effect this week.
- **Lowering** the target takes effect next week. This stops someone dropping their target on Saturday to make the attack land. The UI says "Takes effect Monday".
- **Suggested target** for W: the median number of qualifying runs per week over the 8 completed non-rest weeks before W, rounded, with a minimum of 1.
- **No stored target yet:** the provisional target for W is the **highest** weekly suggestion over the 26 weeks before W, counting only weeks from launch onward. These are the ledger's `suggested` values. For launch week itself, it's that week's own suggestion.
  - It only goes up in the short term, so an unset target still catches a fade.
  - The cell shows the label "suggested" until the user taps "Lock it in" or sets a value in SettingsDrawer.
  - No background job writes targets.
- **Bump prompt:** if the median of the last 4 completed weeks is higher than the stored target, the cell offers "You've been at 5. Bump your Rhythm to 5?"

**Rest weeks.** A rest week pauses everything. You don't attack, you don't block the attack, and your weapon and streak stay where they are.

- **Race rest weeks** are the week containing a `user_goals.race_date` and the week after it.
  - They're written to `rhythm_rest_weeks` with `reason = 'race'` when the goal is saved, and only if the race week hasn't started yet. The write happens in the goals create and update routes.
  - They're kept if the goal is later deleted, and they don't count toward the manual limit.
- **Manual rest weeks** can only be set for the current week, and cleared while it's in progress.
  - They're limited to 1 per 4 weeks, counting the current week and the 3 before it, and counting only rows where `reason = 'manual'`. The limit is enforced in the API.
  - Setting one on a Sunday to save a week is allowed on purpose. It works like a Duolingo streak freeze.
- Stored in `rhythm_rest_weeks(athlete_id bigint, week_start date, reason text not null check (reason in ('race','manual')), created_at timestamptz default now(), primary key (athlete_id, week_start))`.

**Completed-week states**

| State | Rule |
|---|---|
| `rest` | a rest week (checked first) |
| `on` | qualifying runs ≥ target |
| `short` | 1 ≤ runs < target |
| `zero` | 0 runs |

A week that is `short` or `zero` counts as a **miss**.

**Weapon.** Each runner carries a weapon level `s`, measured in weeks. Their tier, and the attack power that comes with it, is read from `s`:

| `s` | Weapon | Power |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Unarmed | 0 |
| 1 | Stick | 1 |
| 2–3 | Dagger | 2 |
| 4–6 | Sword | 3 |
| 7–11 | Axe | 5 |
| 12+ | Legendary | 8 |

`s` is updated once per week. It's stored as `rhythm_weeks.level` and capped at 12.

- The fold starts at `RHYTHM_LAUNCH_WEEK` with `s = 0`: **everyone starts Unarmed on launch day**.
- Frozen weeks read `s` straight from the ledger. Open weeks fold forward from the latest frozen row.
- A runner who joins RunDash after launch starts at their first week with an account.

- **`on`:** `s = s + 1`.
- **`rest`:** `s` doesn't change. Rest weeks are invisible to the miss and redemption logic below.
- **Miss:** the weapon **drops one tier**. `s` becomes the lowest level of the tier below the current one: Legendary → 7, Axe → 4, Sword → 2, Dagger → 1, Stick → 0. A miss while Unarmed stays at 0.
- **Redemption:** the next non-rest week after a **single** miss can undo the drop.
  - It applies only when the week before that miss was `on` (or the fold start).
  - It applies only if no redemption has been used in the previous 4 non-rest weeks.
  - If that next week is `on`, the weapon goes back to `s = sBeforeMiss`, with **no +1**: the week you redeemed buys your weapon back but doesn't also grow it.
- **Two misses in a row:** the first drop stays, the second miss drops another tier, and there's no redemption.
- **Why alternating hits and misses can't climb:** redemption adds nothing, and a second miss within 4 weeks can't be redeemed. So someone who alternates weeks goes down over time, not up.
- `redeemAvailable` for the current week is true only when all of the redemption conditions above are met.

**Rhythm streak.** A separate, plain counter shown on the dashboard: the number of consecutive `on` weeks, skipping rest weeks. It resets to 0 on any miss. Redemption restores the weapon, not the streak count.

**Current-week status** (for display). Check these rules in order; the first one that matches wins.

| # | Status | Rule | Color |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `rest` | this week is a rest week | `--text-3` |
| 2 | `on` | runs ≥ target | `--success` |
| 3 | `at_risk` | `needed > daysLeft`, where `daysLeft` counts today only if there's no qualifying run today yet | `--warning` |
| 4 | `fade` | at least 2 of the last 3 completed non-rest weeks were misses. This catches two misses in a row and alternating weeks. | `--error` |
| 5 | `redeem` | `redeemAvailable` | `--warning` |
| 6 | `building` | everything else: on pace, not done yet | `--text-2` |

**Modules.** There are three layers: pure rules, the ledger writer, and a shared loader.

```
 lib/rhythmData.ts  loadRhythmInputs(athleteIds)       ← dashboard, group page, card route
   ├─ 4 queries total, whatever the group size, each .in('athlete_id', ids), in parallel:
   │   rhythm_weeks, runs, rhythm_targets, rhythm_rest_weeks. Plus the existing
   │   athleteTimezones(ids).
   │   Every query goes through fetchAllPages() from a new shared lib/fetchAllPages.ts.
   │   lib/coachData.ts:17 and lib/analysis/projector-service.ts adopt it too, replacing
   │   their own copies of the same loop (.range() until a short page), so the
   │   1,000-row API cap can never truncate silently.
   │   The runs query uses the earliest window any member needs; each member's rows are
   │   then trimmed to their own window in TS.
   └─ returns RhythmInputs per athlete
 lib/rhythmLedger.ts freezeRhythmWeeks(athleteId)      ← daily cron, and dashboard load when behind
   └─ loads inputs → rhythm.foldWeeks() over weeks ready to freeze → upsert rhythm_weeks
 lib/rhythm.ts       pure rules, no DB
   ├─ qualifyingFloor(runs, week)   qualifyingRuns(runs, week)
   ├─ suggestTarget(ledger, runs, week)   targetForWeek(targets, ledger, week)
   ├─ weekState(...)                foldWeek(prevRows, weekInput) → LedgerRow
   ├─ foldWeeks(fromRow, weeks)     used by BOTH the freezer and the live view
   └─ computeRhythm({ ledger, recentRuns, targets, restWeeks, today }) →
        { target, targetSource, bumpTo, thisWeek: { runs, needed, daysLeft }, status,
          streak, fadeWeeks, weapon: { tier, power, level, nextTier, weeksToNext,
          redeemAvailable }, history }  (history covers the last 12 weeks)
```

`foldWeek` is the only function that applies the weapon rules. The freezer and the live view both call it, so the frozen and live weapon logic can't drift apart.

**UI.** The dashboard gets a Rhythm `.card` (`components/RhythmCell.tsx`), styled from the pre-refresh DESIGN.md on `main`, with a mono uppercase "RHYTHM" label:

- A big mono `2/4`.
- A Mon–Sun row of 7 small chips.
- The status label.
- A small weapon sprite with its tier name and "2 more weeks → Sword".
- The streak count.
- A 12-week history strip.
- "Lock it in", "Bump" and "Rest week" actions when they apply.

No solid orange fill.

**API.**

- `PUT /api/rhythm/target {target}` validates that the target is an integer from 1 to 14, then upserts `rhythm_targets`. Setting or raising takes effect this week; lowering takes effect next week. The Rhythm cell ("Lock it in", "Bump") and SettingsDrawer both call it.
  - `PATCH /api/settings` is **not** touched. It overwrites every profile field on each call (`app/api/settings/route.ts:42`), so sending only the target through it would wipe the rest of the profile.
- `POST`/`DELETE /api/rhythm/rest-week` applies to the current week only.

### Group: Boss Raid

**Group week.** Mon–Sun in `commonTimezone(member zones)`. For group week W, each member contributes their state and weapon for their own week that starts on the same date. Being a few hours out of line across zones is acceptable.

**Active members for week W.** A member is active for W when both hold:

- They were in the group by the start of W, based on `group_members.joined_at`. That column already exists; `app/group/[id]/page.tsx` selects it.
- They have at least one qualifying run in the 8 weeks ending at W's end.

Members who joined mid-week start the following week. Dormant members don't attack, don't block, and show on cards as dim avatars.

**Weekly attack.**

- **Eligible:** active members who are not on a rest week. Their count is `n`.
- **Attackers:** eligible members whose week is `on`.
- **The attack lands** when `attackers ≥ floor(n/2) + 1` (a strict majority), as long as `n ≥ 1`.
- **Damage when it lands:** the sum of every attacker's weapon power *after* this week's upgrade. Otherwise the damage is 0.
- **Blockers:** in a week where the attack fails, the eligible members who missed.
- **MVP:** only when the attack lands. The attacker with the highest power wins; ties go to the longer Rhythm streak, then to name, alphabetically. A failed attack has no MVP, and the card leads with the blockers.
- **Projected damage** (this week): shown as two numbers.
  - **Locked:** the sum of `power(s+1)` over attackers so far.
  - **Possible:** the sum of `power(s+1)` over all eligible members. This is what the attack would deal if everyone still needing runs hits.

**Raids.**

- A raid lasts 4 weeks.
- **Raid boundaries** are every 4 weeks from `RAID_LAUNCH_WEEK = RHYTHM_LAUNCH_WEEK + 7 days`, a code constant. That leaves one hype week in which weapons exist but no boss does. The raid for any week is `floor(weeksSince(RAID_LAUNCH_WEEK) / 4)`.
- **A group's first raid** starts at the first boundary on or after `groups.created_at`. Groups that existed before launch start at `RAID_LAUNCH_WEEK`. No new column is needed.
  - Before the first raid, the Raid panel shows "First boss arrives Mon <date>" alongside everyone's current weapon.
  - No history is backfilled, so there's no "7 bosses slain" on launch day.
  - Check that `groups.created_at` exists in the SQL editor. The table was created outside the repo's migrations. If it doesn't exist, every group starts at `RAID_LAUNCH_WEEK`, and later groups start at the next boundary after their first member joined (`min(group_members.joined_at)`).
- **Boss HP:** fixed at the raid's start as `max(4, round(0.5 × Σ over members active at the raid's start of Σ_{k=1..4} power(s_m + k)))`.
  - That's 50% of the damage the group would deal if every member hit every week. It scales with group size and current weapons.
  - HP is computed from each member's **frozen ledger `level` for the week before the raid**, so it's stable once that week freezes.
  - During the raid's first 2 days (Monday and Tuesday, before the Wednesday freeze), HP is computed from the live fold and marked provisional.
  - HP deliberately includes members who later rest or go dormant. The 0.5 factor is the slack for that: attacks are all-or-nothing and misses cost tiers, so real damage runs well below the maximum (outside-voice finding, eng review).
- **Boss:** v1 has 3 bosses, taking turns in order by `raid_index`: Humidity Golem, Traffic Kraken, Couch Lich. Each has a name, a pixel sprite and 2 taunt lines.
  - v2 grows the roster: Snooze Hydra, Monsoon Wyrm, Excuse Goblin King, Treadmill Mimic, Rest Day Troll.
- **How it ends:** the boss is **slain** in the week cumulative damage reaches its HP. Damage in later weeks of the raid is shown as "overkill" for bragging rights. If HP is still above 0 after 4 weeks, the boss **escapes** and taunts the group on the card.
- **Raid history:** computed from members' frozen ledger rows. It looks back at most 13 raids (a year) for the "bosses slain" count.
  - For that lookback, the group loader reads `rhythm_weeks` rows back to the oldest raid start, at most 52 rows per member.
  - It uses the member list at the time of each read. A member who left no longer counts in past raids, which is accepted.

**Aggregation module.** `lib/raid.ts` holds pure functions:

- `raidWeek(members, week)`, which returns `{ eligible, attackers, landed, damage, blockers, mvp }`
- `raidForWeek(groupId, week)`
- `computeRaid({ groupId, members, groupZone, today })`, which returns `{ boss, hp, damageSoFar, weeks: RaidWeek[], status: 'active' | 'slain' | 'escaped', thisWeek: { eligible, attackersSoFar, needed: Array<{ athlete_id, name, runsNeeded }>, projectedDamage }, lastWeek: RaidWeek | null, bossesSlain }`

The group page and the card route both call `loadRhythmInputs(memberIds)` (see Modules above), plus a read of `group_members` and `groups`. They share one function, `loadRaidState(groupId)`, in `lib/raidData.ts`, so the panel and the card can never disagree.

Reads are bounded: at most 52 ledger rows and about 14 weeks of runs per member. The activities query selects only `athlete_id`, `start_date`, `start_date_local`, `timezone`, `moving_time` and `excluded_from_analysis`, never the `laps` or `streams` jsonb.

**Copy: shaming with a way back.** A template bank lives in `lib/raidCopy.ts`, with lines picked deterministically by `hash(group_id, week, athlete_id)`. There are three levels:

| Situation | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Missed, but the attack landed | gentle | "Sari watched the fight from the bench." |
| Weapon dropped a tier | gentle, pointed | "Budi's Sword shattered. Back to a Dagger." |
| Blocker (the attack failed) | aggressive | "The Couch Lich lives because of Budi and Sari." |

**Every shaming line is followed by a way back**, and the wording has to be true for that person:

- If `redeemAvailable` is true, show the **redemption** line: "Hit your Rhythm this week and get your Sword back."
- Otherwise, including after a second miss or when their tier didn't change, show the **comeback** line: "Hit your Rhythm this week to start climbing again (Dagger in 2 weeks)."

**Shame slots.** The card shows at most 3 names, filled in this order:

1. Blockers.
2. Tier drops, with the highest tier lost first.
3. Plain misses.

Ties go by name, alphabetically. Anyone beyond 3 is shown as "+N others".

Tone rules:

- Shame only about showing up, never about pace, weight or body.
- Never shame anyone who is resting or dormant.
- Never use a line that shames someone for 3 weeks in a row. After 2 consecutive shamings, the next line is an encouraging nudge.

The v2 Coach edition can write this copy in its own voice.

**Share cards.** A route at `app/api/groups/[id]/raid/card/route.tsx`.

- **Runtime:** Node (`export const runtime = 'nodejs'`).
- **Access:** checks group membership the same way `app/api/groups/[id]/peek/route.ts` does. The share flow fetches the PNG as a blob, so the image never needs a public URL.
- **Rendering:** `ImageResponse` from `next/og` at 1080×1350.

It supports two kinds:

- **`?kind=report`** (Monday battle report, for the last completed week):
  - The boss sprite and name, and an HP bar going from its previous value to its new one.
  - "ATTACK LANDED · 11 DMG" or "ATTACK FAILED".
  - **The MVP, large, with their weapon sprite and tier.**
  - The other attackers as small avatar and weapon pairs.
  - A shame section: at most 3 people, each with their redemption line.
  - "Raid week 2/4".
  - Variants for **slain** (a victory banner and bosses-slain count) and **escaped** (a boss taunt line).
- **`?kind=rally`** (mid-week):
  - The boss and its HP bar.
  - "Attack needs 2 more: Budi (1 run), Sari (2 runs)".
  - Days left.
  - "Locked 6 · Possible 14".
  - **After the boss is slain mid-raid,** the card becomes an overkill round: "SLAIN in week 2 · overkill 9 so far · keep your weapons climbing". Weapons still level up and the attack still counts toward overkill.
  - **Before the first raid,** it shows the arrival countdown and everyone's weapon.

**Art.** Weapon and boss sprites are 16×16 pixel grids stored as inline SVG in `lib/sprites.ts`, in orange (`#f97316`) and grey on black. The same sprites appear on the dashboard at a small size. Satori renders inline SVG natively, so there's no emoji font to deal with.
